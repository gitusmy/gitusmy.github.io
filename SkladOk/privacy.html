1. Scope and who we are

SkladOk is an iOS app for small-business inventory management, designed for iPhone and iPad. It can also run on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) as a Designed for iPad app. SkladOk has a companion website at https://kranus.com/SkladOk/. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or the website.

In this Policy, “SkladOk”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to the provider of the SkladOk app and website. You can contact us at support@kranus.com.

This Policy is available in English only. The Ukrainian, Russian, and Spanish versions of the website are separate marketing pages and do not translate this Policy.

2. Summary

In short: SkladOk keeps your inventory records on your device. We do not require an account and do not receive your item names, quantities, prices, comments, documents, barcodes, or CSV files through the app.

The app uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to collect limited app-usage and device information. We do not currently access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.

We do not sell your data or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use Firebase Crashlytics, Remote Config, A/B Testing, or Cloud Messaging in the app.

Our marketing pages do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies. Our support page embeds a form hosted by Microsoft Forms, which may process technical information and the information you submit under Microsoft's privacy policy.

3. Inventory data on your device

SkladOk stores inventory data locally on your device (iPhone, iPad, or Mac) using Apple's on-device storage technologies, including SwiftData and SQLite. This data includes item names, barcodes, quantities, prices, stock levels, documents such as receipts, issues, stock counts and revaluations, comments, tags, and app settings.

We do not operate a cloud account, sync service, or server that stores your inventory records. We do not receive this data through the app. Your device backup settings may include app data in backups controlled by you or your backup provider; we do not have access to those backups.

If you delete the app, reset or lose your device, or a backup fails, your data may be lost. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups.

4. Camera access

The app requests camera access only when you choose to scan a barcode. Camera frames are processed on your device to recognize the barcode. We do not receive camera images or video.

You can change camera permission at any time in your device's settings (iOS Settings on iPhone and iPad, System Settings on Mac). If you deny camera access, you can still enter barcodes manually.

5. Analytics and Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC and its affiliates, to understand how the app is used and to improve it.

Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • an app instance ID;
  • device identifiers, such as the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV);
  • device model, operating system version, and app version;
  • language, region, and approximate location derived from your IP address, such as country or city;
  • app sessions and automatic in-app events such as app opens;
  • technical information about app usage and interactions.

We configure Firebase Analytics so that it does not include the content of your inventory, including item names, quantities, prices, comments, barcodes, CSV contents, or support messages. We do not set a user ID in Firebase Analytics.

We use this information to understand aggregate usage, diagnose technical issues, improve features, and measure the effectiveness of our marketing.

Google acts as our service provider for Firebase Analytics under Google's Firebase Data Processing Terms. Google may process analytics data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and Firebase Data Processing Terms.

6. IDFA, ATT, and campaign measurement

We do not currently access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. We do not use IDFA to display third-party advertising in the app, to remarket to you, or to sell your data.

If we introduce campaign measurement that requires IDFA in a future version, we will update this Policy, request permission through ATT where required, and explain how to change your permission in system settings. The app remains fully functional if tracking is not allowed.

7. Your analytics rights

The app does not currently provide an in-app analytics switch.

You can contact us at support@kranus.com to ask questions about analytics or to exercise your privacy rights. Because we do not require an account or set a user ID, we may need additional information to locate analytics data associated with your app instance. We will use reasonable efforts to respond as required by law.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

SkladOk may offer paid features or subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Apple processes purchases and payments. We do not receive your payment card details.

Apple may provide us with limited information about a transaction, such as the product purchased, transaction status, and subscription status, so we can unlock features and manage access. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

9. Barcode database

The app can retrieve product information from an open barcode database hosted on GitLab at gitlab.com/main-k/skladok-barcodes. The database is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

When the app updates its barcode catalog, it downloads a manifest and data files from GitLab. That request may include technical information such as your IP address. The app does not send your inventory records or barcode searches to GitLab. GitLab and the database host may process technical information under their own privacy policies, and we do not control their independent processing.

Barcode data is provided for convenience and may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. You are responsible for verifying product information before relying on it.

10. CSV import and export

You can import CSV files from a URL you provide or a file you choose on your device, and export data using the system file export or share interface. The app processes these files on your device.

If you import from a third-party URL, that provider may receive your request and technical information such as your IP address. We do not control that provider's processing. Exported files are under your control; share them only with trusted recipients.

11. Support communications

If you email support@kranus.com or submit the support form, we receive the information you provide. The support form asks for your name, email address, type of request, message, consent, and the platform you select. If you email us, we may also receive any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to you, provide support, and improve the app.

The support form is hosted by Microsoft Forms. Microsoft may process technical information and may set cookies when you use the form, under Microsoft's privacy policy.

Please do not send sensitive inventory or business information unless it is necessary for your request. We retain support communications for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them.

12. Our website

Our marketing pages are static informational pages. We do not set cookies, use analytics, or use tracking technologies on those pages. Firebase Analytics is used only in the app, not on the website. The website uses self-hosted fonts and does not request fonts from third-party font services.

Our support page embeds a form hosted by Microsoft Forms. When you open or submit the form, Microsoft may set cookies and process technical information and the information you submit under Microsoft's privacy policy. We use the form to receive and respond to support requests.

The web server that delivers the site may process standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this information to identify or track you.

If you email us or submit the support form, we receive the information you provide. Links to third-party sites, including the App Store, GitLab, Google, and Microsoft, are governed by those third parties' privacy policies.

Because our marketing pages do not track visitors, they do not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. The embedded Microsoft Forms form is governed by Microsoft's practices.

13. Retention

Firebase Analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months, depending on the retention period configured in our Firebase project. Aggregated reports and statistics that do not identify you may be retained longer.

Support communications and support form submissions are retained as described above. Inventory data remains on your device until you delete it or delete the app; we do not hold a copy.

We may retain information longer if required by law or if necessary for security, fraud prevention, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Inventory data is stored on your device and protected by your device's security features. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

You should use a device passcode or login password, keep iOS, iPadOS, or macOS updated, and protect your backups. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. International transfers

Google and Microsoft may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. Google processes analytics data; Microsoft processes support form data. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses and the providers' data processing terms.

You can contact us for more information about the safeguards we use.

16. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact us at support@kranus.com. We may need to verify your request. Because we do not require an account or set a user ID, we may need additional information to locate analytics data associated with your app instance.

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

17. Children

SkladOk is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16, or the minimum age required by the law in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support@kranus.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
