02 Features

Everything you need to keep your stock in order.

No dozens of modules or complicated menus. Just what you do every day.

01 · Scanning

One scan and the item is in the document.

The iPhone camera reads the barcode and SkladOk pulls the name and price from your catalog. If the code is damaged or missing from the label, enter it manually.

  • Camera scanning
  • Manual barcode entry
  • Search by name or barcode
Enter manually

02 · Documents

Receipts, issues, stock counts, revaluations.

Handle every warehouse operation in one place. Drafts, statuses, comments and color tags help you keep track of what matters.

  • Four document types
  • Statuses: draft, deferred, completed
  • Comments and color tags
