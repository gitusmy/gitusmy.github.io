Frequently Asked Questions

What is Card AI flashcards app?

Card AI is a vocabulary builder and flashcards app with AI generation and spaced repetition to help you memorize words, phrases and languages faster.

How does spaced repetition work?

Smart repetition schedules reviews right before you forget — increasing intervals for memorized cards and shortening them for difficult words, maximizing retention.

Can I create my own vocabulary notebooks?

Yes — create custom collections, import/export via Excel, share with friends and generate AI flashcards from any text or topic.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, learn anywhere offline. Your vocabulary notebooks, audio playback and statistics are available without internet.
